# Calibrating Dr. PD

Dr. PD comes calibrated from the factory for accurate voltage and current measurements. However, if you find that the readings are not accurate or want to fine-tune the calibration to match other instruments in your lab, you can follow the steps below to calibrate Dr. PD.

::::warning
The calibration affects the accuracy of _all_ measurements, including the ones that are used to trigger Dr. PD's safety features, such as overvoltage and overcurrent protection. Incorrect calibration may cause Dr. PD to fail to trigger these safety features when they are needed, which could lead to damage to your equipment or even personal injury. Always double-check your calibration settings and ensure that they are correct before using Dr. PD for any measurements.
::::

## How calibration works

Dr. PD's calibration works by linear interpolation between a series of reference intervals (1V for voltage and 500mA for current) that are stored in the device's flash memory. Calibration works by measuring the difference between the actual and measured voltage at the reference intervals, and then calculating a correction factor that is applied to all subsequent measurements.

Therefore, in order to change the calibration coefficients, all you need to is place the device in a known voltage or current condition, and then ask Dr. PD to make a measurement. Dr. PD will then calculate the correction factor based on the difference between the actual and measured voltage or current, and store it in flash memory for future use.

## Preparing protection thresholds

Calibration uses the same measurements that drive overvoltage and overcurrent
protection. Before either voltage or current calibration:

1. Select **Protection → Set thresholds...** to open **OVP/OCP settings**.
2. Record the normal OVP and OCP values so you can restore them afterward.
3. Set OVP above the highest voltage point and OCP above the highest current
   point you intend to calibrate, while keeping both thresholds within the safe
   limits of the reference source, load, cabling, and Dr. PD.

Do not set protection higher than the test setup can safely withstand. An OVP
or OCP trip interrupts calibration.

## Preparing for voltage calibration

1. In order to calibrate Dr. PD's voltage measurement, you will need to first disconnect any devices and cables from both USB-C ports. 
2. Then, connect a known voltage source to the banana jacks, respecting the correct polarity. This voltage source will act as your reference standard for the calibration, and should therefore be accurate and stable. The voltage source should be able to provide a voltage within the range of Dr. PD's measurement capabilities (up to 60V).

## Performing voltage calibration

Select **Device → [paired device] → Calibrate → Voltage...** for the
connected Dr. PD. The app shows a **Calibration warning**, then temporarily
switches Dr. PD to Observer mode and restores its previous mode when the dialog
closes. The voltage table contains points from 0 V through 60 V in 1 V steps.

To update a point, apply the corresponding voltage to the banana jacks and click
**Calibrate** beside that nominal value. If the live measurement is far from the
selected point, the app opens **Confirm calibration point**. Cancel and verify
the reference setup unless the mismatch is expected. Dr. PD then measures the
voltage, calculates the correction, and stores it in flash.

## Preparing for current calibration

Current calibration follows a slightly different procedure:

1. Connect a known-good USB-C source to Port 1.
2. Select **Mode → Sink**, then use **Mode → Choose power contract...** to
   negotiate at least 5 V and enough advertised current for the highest point
   you intend to calibrate. A 5 A contract is needed only for the 5 A point.
3. Connect a known current sink, such as a programmable electronic load, to the
   banana jacks.
4. Confirm that OCP is above the intended calibration current but still within
   the safe limit of every component in the setup.

## Performing current calibration

Select **Device → [paired device] → Calibrate → Current...** for the
connected Dr. PD. The app shows a **Calibration warning** and a table containing
points from 0 A through 6 A in 0.5 A steps.

Set the current sink to the selected nominal current, then click **Calibrate**.
If the live measurement is far from the selected point, the app opens
**Confirm calibration point**. Cancel and verify the load and reference setup
unless the mismatch is expected. Dr. PD then measures the current, calculates
the correction, and stores it in flash.

::::info[Limitations of current calibration]
With this mechanism, you can only calibrate your device up to 5A, which is the maximum current that can be negotiated over USB-C. Higher currents require a custom rig that provides up to 6A on Port 1, which is not available to the general public for safety reasons. Please contact us if you are interested in performing high-current calibration, and we will be happy to assist you.
::::

## Restoring protection

When calibration is complete, select **Protection → Set thresholds...** and
restore the normal OVP and OCP values recorded before calibration. Verify the
calibrated readings against the reference instruments before reconnecting a
device under test. Do not leave elevated calibration thresholds active for
normal operation.
