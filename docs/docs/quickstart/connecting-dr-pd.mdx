# Connecting Dr. PD to your computer or tablet

Dr. PD connects to your computer or tablet using a USB-C cable that supports USB 2.0 communications. The device is powered and controlled through this connection, which also allows it to send captured data to the host software.

## Starting the control software

Before connecting Dr. PD to your computer, make sure that either Chrome or Edge are installed and up to date. Dr. PD runs on most operating systems and does not require drivers or installation, but it does require a compatible browser in order to access the control software.

With your browser running, plug the USB-C cable into the control port at one end and one of the USB ports on your computer or tablet at the other. The device will power on and should be detected by your operating system.

Your browser may show a USB device notification when Dr. PD is connected. You can use that notification to open the control software, or you can open it manually by [navigating to it](https://t76.org/drpd/app).

If you prefer to launch the control software from its own app window, see
[Installing Dr. PD as an app](../how-to/installing-as-app.mdx).

Proceed to [pairing your device](./pairing-device) to establish a secure connection between Dr. PD and the control software.
