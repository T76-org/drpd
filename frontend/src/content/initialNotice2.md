## Your privacy is safe with us

**We do not track our users, ever.**

Dr. PD uses browser storage only to keep application settings and log data on your device. We do not collect any personal information or usage data from you.

The source code for our client application is freely available on [GitHub](https://github.com/T76-org/drpd/tree/main/frontend). You can install it locally on your own device if you prefer to use Dr. PD without any network connectivity.
