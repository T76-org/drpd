## Important safety notice

> **USB-PD devices can deliver high levels of power, and improper use can result in damage to your devices or even personal injury.**

Dr. PD includes a number of safety features designed to help prevent the inadvertent application of unwanted power to the devices attached to its ports. However, it is ultimately your responsibility to ensure that you are using Dr. PD safely and appropriately.

Before you start using Dr. PD, please read our [safety guidelines](https://t76.org/drpd/docs/important-warnings) to ensure that you understand the risks involved and how to mitigate them.
